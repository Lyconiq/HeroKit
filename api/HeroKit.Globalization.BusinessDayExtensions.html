Remarks

Business days are non-weekend days without a full-day holiday. Half-day holidays count as business days. Methods throw ArgumentOutOfRangeException for years the calendar does not cover and NotSupportedException when the country has no calendar.

CountBusinessDays(DateOnly, DateOnly, Country)

Counts the business days after start up to and including end. A half-day holiday on a weekday, for example the eve (arife) of a Turkish religious holiday, counts as 0.5.

public static decimal CountBusinessDays(this DateOnly start, DateOnly end, Country country)

Parameters

start DateOnly

The start date (not counted).

end DateOnly

The end date (counted if it is a business day).

country Country

The country.

Returns

decimal

The number of business days; negative when end is earlier.

Examples

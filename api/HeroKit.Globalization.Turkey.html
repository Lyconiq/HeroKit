GibInvoiceNumber

A Turkish e-Fatura or e-Arşiv invoice number in the GİB format: a 3-character series, a 4-digit year and a 9-digit sequence, for example "ABC2026000000001".

Tckn

A Turkish identity number (T.C. Kimlik Numarası) that has passed the checksum check. Validate once at the edge, then pass the value around.

# Privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. [Türkçe](tr/privacy.md)

This notice covers herokit.lyconiq.com only, the documentation site of the HeroKit library. The site has no accounts, forms or payments.

## What we measure

We use Umami, which we run on our own server (Hetzner, Germany). Requests to it pass through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address while it carries them. The measurement data is stored only on our server. We collect aggregate usage counts:

- page views, the referring address, country, device and browser type, and browser language
- how often the install command is copied and the GitHub and NuGet links are clicked
- the campaign tag in the link (UTM)

No name, email address or account ID is sent. Other parts of the address, such as search terms, are removed before anything is sent.

## Cookies and identifiers

The measurement sets no cookie and leaves no lasting marker in your browser. To count visitors it derives a digest from your request that changes every month. Your IP address is used only for a moment to find your country and is not stored. The data is not tied back to an individual, and nothing follows you between sites.

## Why and for how long

We measure to see which pages are read and how people reach the package. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the data controller (KVKK art. 5/2-f). Raw visit records are deleted after 25 months.

## Other services

The site itself is hosted on the same server, without Cloudflare. Its server logs may record your IP address. The badges and images on the home page load from img.shields.io and raw.githubusercontent.com, so those services see your request.

## Contact

The data controller is the founder who operates Lyconiq. Send questions and requests about your rights to admin@lyconiq.com.

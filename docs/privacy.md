# Privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. [Türkçe](tr/privacy.md)

This notice covers herokit.lyconiq.com only, the documentation site of the HeroKit library. The site has no accounts, forms or payments.

## What we measure

We collect aggregate usage counts: page views, counts of key steps, the referring address, country, device and browser type, browser language, and the campaign tag in the link (UTM). No name, email address or account ID is sent.

The measurement sets no cookie and leaves no lasting marker in your browser. Your IP address is not stored in the measurement data. The data is not tied back to an individual, and nothing follows you between sites.

## Purpose, legal basis and retention

We measure to see which pages are read and how people reach the package. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the data controller (KVKK art. 5/2-f). Raw visit records are deleted after 25 months.

## Transfers

The site and the measurement run through hosting and network providers abroad. These providers see your IP address while they carry the request. The badges and images on the home page load from external services, which also see your request.

## Contact

The data controller is the founder who operates Lyconiq. Send questions and requests about your rights to admin@lyconiq.com.
